About.

I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at NIT Calicut (Class of 2027), working at the intersection of supply chain systems, operations management, and structured analytical problem-solving.

My approach is end-to-end. I focus on understanding the full flow of materials and processes rather than isolated steps, identifying where bottlenecks form and what metrics would make performance legible. During my current internship at Micron Group of Industries, I have been applying this on an active shop floor, supporting WMS implementation and contributing to inventory operations as part of the plant team.

I founded The War Room, a monthly analytical discussion forum at NIT Calicut focused on global developments, macroeconomics, trade policy, and industrial strategy. The format is structured debate and multi-perspective breakdown, oriented toward building reasoning depth rather than surface-level commentary.

I also publish a weekly newsletter on LinkedIn covering global supply chain developments, trade shifts, and logistics trends, aimed at building a consistent habit of tracking how the world moves goods and why it matters.

Beyond this, I have managed budgets exceeding ₹1,00,000 as Treasurer for the Skylight Interdepartment Exhibition, represented the Centre of Excellence in Logistics and Supply Chain Management as a Student Ambassador, and contributed across research, consulting, and operations contexts at the institute level.

I am drawn to work where systems thinking meets execution ownership, supply chain strategy, operations research, and the analytical layer that connects the two.
